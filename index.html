The lock

Hard enough to work. Honest enough to trust.

Not another Ignore Limit. Not a cage you uninstall at 11pm. A lock tied to promises you already made.

  • ✓Default-deny. Every app locks except essentials and a few exceptions you name.
  • ✓Escape but at a cost.
  • ✓Calls never blocked. Phone, messages, emergency, maps, camera stay hard-wired open.
  • ✓Cheating is visible. Kill the permission and the day is unprotected — it won’t count.

Meet Sprig

Eight forms. Earned, never bought.

Keep the streak → Sprig evolves. Miss without a shield → streak resets. Every form you’ve earned stays in your gallery.

Weekly looksEach week has its own colour and accessory. Same form, different week = different card.

One shield a weekForgives one miss. Refills Monday.

Wilt and reviveBreak the lock → Sprig wilts until tomorrow’s habits are done. A buddy cheer revives early.
